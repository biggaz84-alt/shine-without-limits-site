Ordering, Payment & Safety

Ordering Pricing Payment Safety

How ordering works

Orders are currently handled by email so every product and custom request can be checked properly before payment. This is especially useful for custom PECS-style cards, coming soon routine boards and personalised support bundles.

  • Choose a product, build a PECS card set or send a custom request.
  • Email your order details or use one of the website enquiry buttons.
  • We confirm the details, final price, postage and timescale.
  • Payment details are sent after everything is agreed.

Photo cards are not currently available. We focus on clear, bold visual cards with simple symbols and wording so they print cleanly and stay easy to understand. Final price, postage and timescale are always confirmed by email before your order is agreed.

PayPal payment

Payment is taken by PayPal after your order details, postage and final total have been confirmed by email. Please do not send payment until your order has been checked and agreed.

Once your order is confirmed, payment can be made securely through PayPal.

Delivery

Delivery times can vary depending on the product, quantity and whether the order is custom-made. A timescale will be confirmed before payment.

  • Estimated timescale confirmed by email.
  • Postage cost confirmed before payment.
  • Custom orders may take longer than ready-made items.
  • Delivery details should be checked carefully before the order is made.

Returns & custom items

If there is a problem with your order, please get in touch so it can be looked into. Custom-made items are created to your requested details, so returns may be limited unless there is a fault.

  • Ready-made items may be returnable if unused and in suitable condition.
  • Custom-made or personalised items may not be returnable unless faulty.
  • Any issue should be reported as soon as possible after delivery.
  • Please keep photos of any damage or issue so it can be checked properly.

Product safety

Some sensory items, bag tags, keyrings and small products may contain small parts or be unsuitable for unsupervised use.

  • Adult supervision is recommended where appropriate.
  • Products are not intended to be chewed unless clearly stated.
  • Check items regularly and stop use if damaged.
  • Small parts may not be suitable for children under 3 years old.
  • Colours and finish may vary slightly on handmade or 3D printed items.

Custom PECS cards

Custom PECS-style cards can be made for routines, emotions, places, food choices, school needs, days out, toilet training and family-specific communication.

  • Choose from ready-made options.
  • Add your own words, symbols or ideas.
  • Build your own set using the PECS card builder.
  • Ask by email if you need a card that is not listed.

Before you pay

Please wait for confirmation before sending any PayPal payment. This helps make sure the correct products, postage and custom details are agreed first.

  • Order checked by email first.
  • Final total confirmed before payment.
  • PayPal link supplied for easy payment.
  • No order is started until the details are confirmed.

Need help before ordering?

Send a message with what you need and we’ll help work out the best option. You can ask about PECS-style cards, custom bundles, routine boards, sensory items, clothing interest or personalised support.
