Customer information

Delivery & Returns

Clear order, postage and returns information for Shine Without Limits customers.

Postage

Standard UK postage is currently £3.99 per order unless a different postage option is needed. Any change to postage will be confirmed before payment.

Making and dispatch times

Ready-made bundles usually dispatch within 3–5 working days. Custom cards usually take 5–10 working days after wording and design details are confirmed. If you need an order for a specific date, please mention this before ordering.

Order confirmation

Before anything is made, we confirm the product, quantity, custom wording, price, postage and estimated timescale by email.

Damaged or faulty items

If your order arrives damaged or faulty, please contact us as soon as possible with your order details and clear photos of the issue so we can help.

Returns

Ready-made items may be returnable if unused and in original condition. Custom or personalised items are made specifically for your order and may not be returnable unless faulty or not as agreed.

Cancellations

If you need to cancel, please contact us quickly. Once a custom order has started printing or making, cancellation may not always be possible.

Questions

For delivery, returns or order questions, email shinewithoutlimits@hotmail.com.
