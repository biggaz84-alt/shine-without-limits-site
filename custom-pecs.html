Payment

Payment is made after your card choices, final total and postage are confirmed. We can send a secure Stripe card payment link or invoice once your final total is confirmed.

Making time

Ready-made card selections usually dispatch within 3-5 working days. Custom wording or symbols usually take 5-10 working days after details and payment are confirmed.

Schools & SEN settings

Written quotes are available for schools, nurseries and SEN settings. Purchase order requests can be discussed before ordering.

These are visual communication support items for supervised use. They are not toys, teethers, chew products, medical devices or official PECS® training materials. See our safety information before ordering.

Photo cards are not currently available. We focus on clear, bold visual cards with simple symbols and wording so they print cleanly and stay easy to understand.
