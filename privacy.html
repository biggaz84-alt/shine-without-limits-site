Customer information

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This page explains how Shine Without Limits handles customer information when you contact us, ask about products, or place an order.

Who we are

Shine Without Limits creates 3D printed visual communication support cards and related custom visual supports. You can contact us at shinewithoutlimits@hotmail.com.

Information we may collect

When you contact us or place an order, we may collect your name, email address, delivery details, postcode, product choices, custom wording, payment confirmation information, and messages you send to us.

Custom order information

If you request custom cards, you may choose to send wording, routine details, preferred colours, names or other information needed to make the product. Please only send information you are happy for us to use to complete your order.

How we use your information

We use your information to reply to enquiries, confirm order details, make and deliver products, handle payments, provide customer support, and keep basic records for business and tax purposes.

Children's information

Our customers may order visual supports for children. We only ask for information needed to make the order. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. If a custom card needs a name or specific wording, we will use it only for that order.

Sharing information

We do not sell customer information. We may share limited information with delivery providers, payment services, or professional advisers where needed to complete orders, manage payments or meet legal obligations.

How long we keep information

We keep order and contact information only for as long as needed for customer service, records, legal obligations and reasonable business purposes.

Your rights

You can contact us to ask what information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask for information to be deleted where we no longer need to keep it.

Important note

This is a practical small-business privacy policy draft. It should be reviewed and updated as Shine Without Limits grows, especially if online checkout, account creation or photo uploads are added later.
