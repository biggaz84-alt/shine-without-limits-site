About Shine Without Limits

A small family-led UK project with a practical purpose

Shine Without Limits was created by Gareth and Vicky, a UK family with lived experience of autism, visual supports and everyday communication needs.

Vicky works alongside non-verbal children in the care community and saw how useful laminated visual cards could be, but also how easily they could fold, peel, get soggy or become damaged through everyday handling. She asked Gareth if he could try making a stronger 3D printed version.

What started with simple cards like toilet, drink and park has grown into a small family-led project shaped by real families, care settings, friends and early customers.

From one parent to another, the aim is simple: clearer communication, calmer routines and visual cards that can handle real life.

We are not speech therapists and we do not provide diagnosis, therapy, behaviour plans or official PECS training. Our role is simple: creating practical 3D printed visual support items for families, schools, nurseries and SEND settings to use alongside their own routines and professional guidance where needed.
