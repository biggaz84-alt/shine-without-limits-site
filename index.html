How we price 3D printed cards

Our visual cards are 3D printed in-house from PLA+ filament and finished with rounded edges for a smooth feel. Pricing is straightforward: single cards start from £2.00, bundles from £8.99.

We confirm the total before making anything. Standard UK postage is currently £3.99 per order unless your order needs a different postage option. Ready-made bundles usually dispatch within 3–5 working days, while custom orders usually take 5–10 working days after details are confirmed.

About Shine Without Limits

Helping every child shine without limits

Shine Without Limits is a small family-led project creating bright, practical visual supports for children, families and everyday routines. Our focus is durable, 3D printed visual communication cards that support communication, choices, emotions and daily structure.

We do not offer speech therapy, diagnosis or official PECS training. We simply create practical visual support products that families and settings can use alongside their own routines, guidance and professional support where needed.
