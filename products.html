How do I order?

Yes. Custom cards can be made for routines, places, emotions, food choices, school needs and family life.

How much are PECS cards?

Standard ready-made visual cards are £2.00 each. Bundles start from £8.99, with 10 cards for £16.99, 15 cards for £23.99 and 20 cards for £29.99. Postage and final details are confirmed by email.

📦

How long does an order take?

Times can vary depending on the product and custom details. Timescale, postage and payment are confirmed by email.
