Simple ordering plan

How ordering will work

🎨

1. Choose a design

Pick from the Shine Wear preview designs or ask about a custom clothing idea.

👕

2. Pick clothing type

Choose t-shirt or hoodie, depending on the design and available options.

📏

3. Choose size

T-shirts are planned from Small to 4XL. Hoodies are planned from Small to 5XL.

✅

4. Confirm before making

We’ll confirm the final total, availability, payment option and timescale before anything is made.

Interested in Shine Wear?

If you’d like to ask about one of the preview designs, send us a quick message with the design name, clothing type, size and colour preference.
