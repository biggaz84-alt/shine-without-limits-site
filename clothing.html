How to order Shine Wear

🎨

1. Choose a design

Pick from the Shine Wear t-shirt designs or ask about adding a name or short custom wording.

👕

2. Pick your t-shirt details

Choose the size and one of the available colours: White, Black, Purple or Light Pink.

📏

3. Choose size

T-shirts are available from Small to 4XL. Hoodies and sweatshirts are coming soon as the range grows.

✅

4. Confirm before making

Standard t-shirts are £14.99, personalised t-shirts start from £16.99, and we’ll confirm the final total before anything is made.

Order or ask about Shine Wear

If you’d like to ask about one of the t-shirt designs, send us a quick message with the design name, size, colour choice and whether you would like a name or custom wording added. Available t-shirt colours are White, Black, Purple and Light Pink.
